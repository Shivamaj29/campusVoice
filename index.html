Context & Landscape

Problem Statement

Traditional manual academic dispute resolution and complaint lodging mechanisms in educational institutions suffer from major structural and communication bottlenecks.

Manual Handling & Paper Trails

Complaints and reviews require tedious, hand-written submission forms. Physical documents are easily lost, misplaced, or forgotten on desks.

Zero Tracking & Updates

Students have no visual or real-time method to track the progress of their filed grievances or academic reviews once submitted.

No Notifications or Alerts

Absence of automated notifications leaves students and teaching authorities completely unaware of status changes, assignments, or escalations.

Delayed Resolution (No Escalation)

When an intermediate authority fails to act on a concern, the system lacks automatic routing structures to escalate the matter to senior heads.

Poor Integrity & Security

Open paper formats lead to breaches of student privacy, allowing unauthorized individuals to read, tamper with, or dismiss sensitive issues.

No Analytical Insights

College heads and HODs have no access to structured data. It is impossible to identify persistent departmental challenges or faculty review spikes.

Vision & Roadmap

System Objectives

The primary goal of the system is to establish a fast, high-integrity, and automated digital workspace for resolving diverse student complaints and review petitions.

Digital Complaint Hub

Completely digitize grievance registrations with secure authentication, categorized tagging, and optional file attachments.

Transparent Workflow

Keep students fully informed of who is handling their grievance, current department assignment, and active status updates.

Auto Escalation

Enforce strict resolution SLA windows. If a level is idle, automatically escalate to the next hierarchical tier.

Real-Time Analytics

Equip college administration and HODs with actionable visual metrics on response rates and unresolved problems.

Policy & Compliance

Business Rules

Strict system validations and operational rules designed to preserve academic integrity and response timelines.

When a student registers a grievance, the system validates eligibility and targets departmental scope:

  • Unique Case Identifiers: Every single complaint automatically receives a structured ticket tag (e.g., GR-2026-8841).
  • Single Classification Rule: A grievance can only reside in one primary department classification at a time (preventing collision).
  • Visibility Partition: Students can only view their own lodged history, while faculties only see assigned cases.

Escalation routines protect students from neglected tickets. Each operational level operates on strict timeframe limits:

  • Teacher (Level 1): Must log a reply/remark or action within 48 Hours.
  • Tutor Guardian (Level 2): Receives breach tickets on Day 3. Must address within 72 Hours.
  • Class Incharge (Level 3): Case escalates automatically on Day 6. Allowed 48 Hours.
  • HOD (Level 4): Direct management on Day 8. Allowed 72 Hours before final routing.
  • Committee (Level 5): Board decision on Day 11. Concludes case with irreversible finality.

The messaging pipeline routes push notifications instantly when state transitions take place:

  • Ticket Registration: Dispatches confirmation email to student and alert to L1 Teacher.
  • Authority Assignments: Notifies assigned handler with ticket metadata and SLA deadline.
  • Escalation Alarms: Flags parent department HOD and flags active breach level.
